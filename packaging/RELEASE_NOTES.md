The Motion Radar helper for Windows 10 and 11.

1. Download `MotionRadar-windows.zip` below and unzip it anywhere.
2. Open `MotionRadar\MotionRadar.exe`. If Windows says it protected your PC, choose
   **More info**, then **Run anyway** (the app is not code-signed).
3. The dashboard opens at https://devadit1515.github.io/wifi-motion-radar/ and connects
   to the helper. If the browser asks to allow access to apps on this device, allow it.

Keep the helper's window open while sensing; closing it stops sensing and frees the
microphone. Without internet, the same dashboard is at http://127.0.0.1:8765.
Recordings and settings stay in `%LOCALAPPDATA%\MotionRadar` on your computer.
